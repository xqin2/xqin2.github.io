I am a Ph.D. student in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the California Institute of Technology, advised by Professor Thomas Y. Hou. I received my undergraduate degree from Peking University in 2024.

My research interests include nonlinear partial differential equations in fluid dynamics, singularity formation, self-similar dynamics, and computer-assisted proofs.
