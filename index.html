I am a Ph.D. student in Applied and Computational Mathematics at the California Institute of Technology, advised by Professor Thomas Y. Hou. I received my undergraduate degree from Peking University in 2024.

At present, my main research interests lie in nonlinear partial differential equations arising in fluid dynamics, especially singularity formation, self-similar dynamics, and computer-assisted proofs. I am always happy to exchange ideas and welcome collaborations on a broad range of mathematical problems.
