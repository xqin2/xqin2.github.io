Research

My research is in nonlinear partial differential equations and mathematical fluid dynamics. I am particularly interested in singularity formation, self-similar solutions, and the analysis of nonlocal evolution equations.

  • Incompressible fluid equations
  • Singularity formation and self-similarity
  • Nonlocal transport equations
